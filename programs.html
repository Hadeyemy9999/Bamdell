Special Needs Caregiving

The 7 Groups of Children We Care For

Providing continuous clinical rehabilitation, nurturing warmth, shelter, and educational pathways:

👶

1. Motherless Babies

Infant Intensive Care

Babies who tragically lost their mothers at birth or during early infancy receive round-the-clock maternal attention, specialized baby milk formula, clean nursery housing, pediatric checkups, and loving warmth.

🏠

2. Orphans

Long-term Family Shelter

Providing permanent residential shelter, balanced meals, clothing, schooling, spiritual mentorship, and a family-style environment where children feel secure, loved, and valued.

♿

3. Physically Challenged Children

Physiotherapy & Mobility

Regular physiotherapy sessions, muscle strengthening routines, custom-fitted pediatric wheelchairs, orthopedic shoes, crutches, and mobility training to foster daily independence.

🦯

4. Blind / Deaf Children

Sensory Adaptation

Equipping visually impaired children with braille slates, styluses, audio learning devices, and white canes. Teaching Nigerian Sign Language and hearing aid maintenance for deaf children.

💛

5. Children with Down Syndrome

Cognitive Enrichment

Individualized education plans, speech articulation support, motor coordination exercises, social skills integration, and empathetic caregivers who nurture their gifts and talents.

🩺

6. Children with Cerebral Palsy

Intensive Physical Care

Comprehensive care addressing muscle spasticity, posture management, supportive seating, assisted feeding, specialized medical check-ins, and consistent rehabilitation exercises.

🧩

7. Children with Autism / Speech Impairment

Speech & Sensory Therapy

Providing structured sensory-friendly routines, speech therapy sessions, visual communication picture cards (PECS), behavior guidance, and unconditional patience to unlock each child's expressive ability.

Ministering to Spiritual & Physical Needs

How We Help & Care for Our Children

"We minister to their spiritual and physical needs through:"

🍲 🏡

1. Shelter, Food, Clothing & Physiotherapy

A fully equipped home in Ibadan offering clean rooms, continuous nutrition, pediatric medications, clean apparel, and daily physical rehabilitation.

📚 🎓

2. Scholarships for Inclusive Education

Funding school fees, braille materials, special education teacher support, and learning supplies so every child receives quality schooling.

♿ 🦯

3. Mobility Aids & Appliances

Distributing rugged pediatric wheelchairs, crutches, white canes, hearing aids, and orthopedic appliances tailored to each child's body.
