Transformative Programs for Children with Disabilities

We break down barriers to education, mobility, and healthcare through structured, community-centered initiatives tailored to each child's needs.

Education Pillar

1. Inclusive Education & Scholarship Initiative

In Nigeria, less than 10% of children with disabilities attend formal school due to physical barriers, lack of braille/adaptive materials, and teacher training deficits.

Our Inclusive Education program bridges this gap by funding full primary and secondary scholarships, supplying braille slates, audio readers, and sign language interpreters, and retrofitting partner school ramps and toilets.

  • ✓ 500+ Active School Scholarships funded across Lagos and FCT Abuja.
  • ✓ 12 Partner Schools transformed with accessible wheelchair ramps and adaptive desks.
  • ✓ 150+ Teachers Trained in inclusive pedagogy and Universal Design for Learning (UDL).

Health & Rehab

2. Pediatric Rehabilitation & Physiotherapy

Early intervention is crucial for children living with cerebral palsy, spina bifida, and limb impairments.

We operate two free community rehabilitation centers in Lagos (Ikeja) and Abuja (CBD), offering weekly pediatric physiotherapy, occupational therapy, and speech therapy conducted by certified specialists.

  • ✓ 12,000+ Free Therapy Sessions conducted since founding.
  • ✓ Custom Muscle Fitting & Splints provided for joint support.
  • ✓ Home Exercise Kits and caregiver training for continuous home care.
Fund 1 Month of Therapy (₦25,000)
Assistive Aids

3. Custom Assistive Tech & Mobility Aids

Mobility is freedom. Without a proper wheelchair or crutch, a child remains confined indoors, unable to socialize or learn.

We source, measure, and assemble terrain-hardened wheelchairs adapted for rough Nigerian roads, alongside pediatric standing frames, white canes, hearing aids, and digital screen-reader devices.

  • ✓ 380+ Rough-Terrain Wheelchairs distributed directly to children.
  • ✓ Free Repair & Maintenance Clinic offered bi-monthly for all devices.
  • ✓ Digital Tactile Tablets provided for blind secondary school students.
Donate a Custom Wheelchair (₦50,000)
Family Care

4. Parent Empowerment & Community Advocacy

Mothers of children with disabilities in Nigeria frequently face severe social abandonment and financial hardship.

We organize monthly parent support circles and provide micro-grants (₦50,000 - ₦150,000) for mothers to start small-scale petty trading or artisanal businesses, ensuring financial independence and household nutritional security.

  • ✓ 180+ Mothers Supported with micro-business start-up grants.
  • ✓ Community Stigma Eradication Drives reaching 50,000+ residents via radio & town halls.
  • ✓ Legal Advocacy securing compliance with the Discrimination Against Persons with Disabilities (Prohibition) Act.
Partner with Our Family Network

How to Access Services & Eligibility Criteria

We welcome applications from parents, guardians, schools, and hospitals across Nigeria.

Any child aged 0 to 18 living in Nigeria with a verified physical, visual, hearing, speech, or cognitive impairment, and whose family lacks the financial resources to afford specialized education, mobility aids, or rehabilitation therapy.
1. Child's birth certificate or age declaration.
2. Brief medical assessment or diagnosis report from a recognized hospital (if available; otherwise our team provides a free preliminary clinical evaluation).
3. Parent/Guardian National Identification Number (NIN) or voter card.
4. Proof of residence in Lagos, FCT Abuja, or neighboring state.
