Child Protection & Safeguarding Policy

Bam Dell Disabilities and Orphanage Home • Ibadan, Nigeria

1. Zero Tolerance Statement

Bam Dell Disabilities and Orphanage Home maintains an absolute zero-tolerance policy towards any form of child abuse, exploitation, harassment, or neglect. All caregivers, resident staff, volunteers, visiting donors, and contractors must adhere strictly to this policy.

2. Scope & Applicability

This policy applies to all activities conducted at our home located at 5B/5A, Opposite Bolude Hall, Alafara, Olubadan Adeoti, Ibadan, during physical therapy sessions, school transportation, community outings, and digital media handling.

3. Key Principles

  • Child's Best Interests: Every decision made at Bam Dell Home prioritizes the spiritual, physical, emotional, and social safety of our children.
  • Dignified Representation: Media representation must emphasize each child's divine dignity, progress, and capabilities — never in degrading or exploitative formats.
  • Caregiver Background Checks: All staff and long-term volunteers undergo rigorous character and background screening prior to placement.
