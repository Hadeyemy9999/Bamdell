Child Protection & Safeguarding Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2024 • Document Ref: HAFN-POL-003

1. Zero Tolerance Statement

Hope Ability Foundation Nigeria (HAFN) maintains a zero-tolerance policy towards any form of child abuse, exploitation, harassment, or neglect. All staff, volunteers, contractors, board members, and visiting partners must adhere strictly to this policy.

2. Scope & Applicability

This policy applies to all activities conducted at HAFN rehabilitation centers, partner inclusive schools, community field outreaches, home visits, digital communications, and promotional media creation.

3. Key Principles

  • Child's Best Interests: Every decision made by HAFN prioritizes the physical, emotional, and social safety of the child.
  • Informed Consent: No photo, video, or audio recording of a beneficiary is taken or published without prior written consent from the parent/guardian AND assent from the child where age-appropriate.
  • Dignified Representation: Media representation must emphasize the child's strengths, abilities, and human dignity — never portraying them in degrading or pity-centric formats.
