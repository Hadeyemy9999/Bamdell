Our Objectives & Current Needs

Committed to promoting the development, protection, rehabilitation, and inclusive education of special needs children and orphans in Ibadan, Nigeria.

Foundation Mandate

Our Three Guiding Objectives

Guiding every program, donation, and therapy session at Bam Dell Home:

🌱

Promote Development

Objective 1: To promote the development of children with special needs through tailored physiotherapy, sensory integration, cognitive coaching, and comprehensive healthcare.

🛡️

Protection & Rehabilitation

Objective 2: To provide protection, immediate and long-term support, and rehabilitation for motherless babies, orphans, and physically challenged children.

🎓

Education & Self-Reliance

Objective 3: To promote inclusive education, guidance, and empowerment toward independence, dignity, and self-reliance.
