Who We Are

Breaking Barriers for Nigeria's Most Vulnerable Children

Over 2.5 million children in Nigeria live with disabilities, often facing extreme stigma, lack of accessible schooling, and inadequate physical therapy.

Hope Ability Foundation Nigeria (HAFN) works directly at the grassroots level in Lagos, Abuja, and rural communities to provide comprehensive care—from custom wheelchairs and braille kits to pediatric physiotherapy and parent support groups.

Our Core Pillars

  • ✓ Dignity First: Respecting every child's intrinsic worth and rights under the Child Rights Act.
  • ✓ Equal Opportunity: Ensuring zero child is left behind due to physical or financial barriers.
  • ✓ Financial Transparency: 100% audited accounts and published impact reports.
  • ✓ Community Ownership: Partnering with local leaders, teachers, and healthcare workers.
