About Us / Founder's Story

Our Story Begins With Love

My name, Bamidele Oluyinka S., Founder of BAM_DELL Disabilities and Orphanage Home.

I live with a disability. Growing up was not easy. I was rejected, overlooked, and counted out.

But if not for God, and for the mother that stood by me when I was just like the children I care for today, my story would have been very different.

She had no money, but she had faith. She had love. That love kept me alive.

In 2022, God asked me: "Because someone stood by you, will you now stand by others?" That was how BAM_DELL was born.

Today we provide Food, Shelter, Education, Therapy and Dignity to children with Autism, Down Syndrome, Cerebral Palsy, Blindness, Deafness, motherless baby and Orphans.

We believe: My strength is made perfect in weakness. - 2 Corinthians 12:9

Commitment to Care

Who We Serve at Bam Dell Home

"To the Glory of God and His Grace, we are committed to taking good care of the following children with special needs:"

👶

Motherless Babies

Infant nutrition, 24/7 loving maternal nursing, specialized baby formulas, and safe early-childhood shelter.

🏠

Orphans

Permanent shelter, family-style guardianship, clothing, spiritual grounding, and foundational schooling.

♿

Physically Challenged Children

Continuous physiotherapy, customized wheelchairs, pediatric crutches, and mobility rehabilitation.

🦯

Blind / Deaf Children

Braille literacy tools, white canes, Nigerian Sign Language instruction, and sensory adaptation aids.

💛

Children with Down Syndrome

Cognitive enrichment, motor-skill therapy, speech support, and individualized special educational care.

🩺

Children with Cerebral Palsy

Dedicated daily physical rehabilitation, posture correction, assistive seating, and clinical care.

🧩

Children with Autism / Speech Impairment

Speech therapy, structured behavioral routines, sensory therapy rooms, and social communication training.

How We Help

Ministering to Spiritual & Physical Needs

"We minister to their spiritual and physical needs through:"

🍲 Shelter, Food, Clothing & Physiotherapy

Providing safe residential housing at our Ibadan center, nutritious daily meals, clean clothing, and dedicated physical rehabilitation therapy.

🎓 Scholarships for Inclusive Education

Covering tuition fees, special learning materials, braille slates, and assistive teaching aids so no child is excluded from learning.

♿ Mobility Aids & Appliances

Distributing pediatric wheelchairs, white canes, crutches, hearing devices, and orthopedic appliances tailored to each child's needs.

Core Objectives

Our Guiding Objectives

  • 🎯
    Promote Child Development:

    To promote the holistic spiritual, cognitive, and physical development of children with special needs.

  • 🛡️
    Protection & Rehabilitation:

    To provide protection, immediate and long-term support, and medical rehabilitation for vulnerable children.

  • 🌱
    Inclusive Education & Self-Reliance:

    To promote inclusive education, mentorship, and empowerment toward independence and self-reliance.

Meet Kingdavid

Before BAM_DELL

When Kingdavid came to us, he couldn't walk well or straighten his body.

He couldn't tell anyone when he needed the toilet, so he would pee and poo on himself.

Water constantly dropped from his mouth, soaking his chest to his stomach.

He came with rashes and skin spots all over his body and head.

He had no control over food and would shout for it all the time.

After BAM_DELL

Today, Kingdavid walks perfectly.

He goes to the toilet by himself.

His skin is clear and his body is shining.

The water mouth has reduced to zero.

He is calm, happy, and learning every day.

This is what happens when Love Leads.
