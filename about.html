About Hope Ability Foundation Nigeria

Dedicated to restoring hope, dignity, and independence for Nigerian children with physical, sensory, and intellectual disabilities.

🎯

Our Mission

To empower children with disabilities and vulnerable youth across Nigeria by providing equitable access to inclusive education, specialized rehabilitation therapy, assistive technology, and family economic empowerment.

👁️

Our Vision

A Nigeria where every child—regardless of disability or socio-economic background—is celebrated, educated, and equipped to reach their full human potential in an inclusive society free from stigma.

Guiding Principles

Our Core Values

👑

Human Dignity

We treat every child as a unique individual worthy of profound respect, rights, and affection.

🤝

Radical Inclusion

We break down physical, societal, and attitudinal barriers that isolate vulnerable children.

🔍

Total Transparency

We publish open financial reports and maintain 100% accountability to our donors and community.

🌱

Sustainability

We build local capacity through teacher training and parent support networks for lasting impact.

Our Journey

How We Came to Be

2019 — The Spark

Founding

Founded in Ikeja, Lagos by special education advocate Dr. Grace Adebayo after recognizing the severe lack of therapeutic support for out-of-school children with cerebral palsy.

2021 — Community Therapy Clinic

Milestone

Opened our first free community pediatric physiotherapy center in Lagos, serving 250+ children in its first year and launching our assistive wheelchair distribution drive.

2023 — Abuja Outreach & Scholarship Expansion

Expansion

Expanded outreach to FCT Abuja and Northern Nigeria, enrolling 500+ blind and visually impaired pupils in inclusive partner schools with braille kits.

2025–2026 — 1,450+ Lives Transformed

Today

Currently supporting over 1,450 children across Nigeria with active physical therapy clinics, scholarship grants, parent micro-grants, and policy advocacy.
