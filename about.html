Our Mission

"To be a loving and positive change in the lives of hopeless orphans and children with disabilities, and to better their tomorrow."

Strategic Goals

Our Primary Objectives

Established to provide holistic care and life-changing intervention for special needs children in Ibadan and across Nigeria:

🌱

1. Promote Development

To promote the holistic development of children with special needs through tailored physiotherapy, sensory engagement, speech therapy, and nutritional care.

🛡️

2. Protection & Rehabilitation

To provide protection, immediate and long-term support, safe residential shelter, and comprehensive physical rehabilitation for vulnerable and abandoned children.

🎓

3. Education & Self-Reliance

To promote inclusive education, vocational guidance, assistive technological literacy, and empowerment toward self-reliance and independence.

Our Family

The Children We Lovingly Care For

"To the Glory of God and His Grace, we are committed to taking good care of the following children with special needs:"

👶 Motherless Babies

Specialized infant feeding, round-the-clock nursing, pediatric oversight, and motherly affection.

🏠 Orphans

Warm family environment, spiritual mentorship, character development, and academic support.

♿ Physically Challenged Children

Physiotherapy sessions, custom wheelchairs, pediatric crutches, and mobility appliances.

🦯 Blind / Deaf Children

Braille slates, audio learning materials, Sign Language training, and white canes.

💛 Children with Down Syndrome

Cognitive development, social interaction, speech coaching, and life skills.

🩺 Children with Cerebral Palsy

Targeted physiotherapy, muscle tone exercises, assistive chairs, and daily caregiver support.

🧩 Children with Autism / Speech Impairment

Speech therapy, calm sensory spaces, communication tools, and routine guidance.
