HAFN Distributes 50 Custom Terrain Wheelchairs in Ikeja, Lagos State

Published: February 10, 2026 • Written by: Communications Team, HAFN

♿ 🎒 🏫

Ikeja, Lagos State — In an emotion-filled event at the Ikeja Inclusive Education Hub, Hope Ability Foundation Nigeria (HAFN) handed over 50 custom, rough-terrain wheelchairs to children living with physical disabilities and motor impairments.

Standard imported wheelchairs often break down quickly on untarred Nigerian roads and high gutters. The mobility aids distributed by HAFN are specially built with reinforced steel frames, puncture-proof thick tires, and adjustable seating to accommodate growing children.

"Without a proper wheelchair, a child is effectively isolated from school, friends, and community life. Today, 50 children have regained their independence and dignity."

— Dr. Grace Adebayo, Executive Director of HAFN

Holistic Support for Beneficiary Families

In addition to receiving mobility devices, each child was presented with a back-to-school kit containing adaptive learning supplies, while their mothers participated in a financial literacy workshop organized by our Parent Micro-Grant Team.

HAFN extends its profound gratitude to our individual donors and corporate partners whose generous contributions made this distribution possible.
