New Mobility Aids & Physiotherapy Care in Ibadan

Published by Bam Dell Disabilities and Orphanage Home • Ibadan, Nigeria

To the Glory of God and His Grace, Bam Dell Disabilities and Orphanage Home continues its daily mission of ministering to the spiritual and physical needs of special needs children, motherless babies, and orphans at our facility in Alafara, Olubadan Adeoti, Ibadan.

Restoring Mobility & Joy

For children living with Cerebral Palsy and physical challenges, access to specialized pediatric wheelchairs and walking frames provides immense freedom and independence. Regular physiotherapy exercises conducted by volunteer clinicians help build muscle tone and coordination.
