Items Needed for Our Children

You can purchase and drop off any of these items, or send funds earmarked specifically for them:

🍚
Food Stuffs

Rice, beans, yam, semo, cooking oil, milk, baby formula (SMA/NAN).

♿
Mobility Aids

Wheelchairs, crutches, walking frames, white canes, orthopedic shoes.

💊
Medication

Multivitamins, analgesics, anticonvulsants, first aid supplies, antiseptics.

👶
Diapers & Wipes

Sizes 1 to 5 diapers, pediatric & youth incontinence pads, baby wipes.

🎓
Scholarships

Inclusive school fees, special education uniforms, braille books.

🏡
Property / Land

Expansion space in Oyo State for therapy suites and youth hostel.

🚿
Water Heater

Electric/solar heaters for infant care and warm physiotherapy baths.

🪑
Furniture

Cots, bunk beds, orthopaedic mattresses, therapy tables, chairs.

⚡
Generator

Power generator or solar inverters for continuous electricity & therapy.

💻
Computers

Laptops, tablets, screen-readers for special education & digital skills.
