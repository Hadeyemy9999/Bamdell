Privacy Policy & Data Protection (NDPA 2023)

Last Updated: January 15, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information necessary to deliver our non-profit services, process donations, and coordinate volunteer activities. This includes:

  • Donor contact details and payment records (processed securely via Paystack).
  • Beneficiary intake information provided by parents/guardians.
  • Volunteer application details and vetting documentation.

2. Use of Personal Data

In accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023), personal data is processed lawfully and transparently to fulfill non-profit service delivery, issue tax receipts, and communicate program updates.

3. Data Security & Storage

We implement encryption and access controls. We do not sell, trade, or rent personal data to third parties under any circumstances.

4. Your Data Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held by HAFN. Contact our Data Protection Officer at dpo@hopeability.org.ng.
