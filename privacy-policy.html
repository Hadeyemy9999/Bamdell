Privacy Policy (NDPA 2023 Compliant)

Bam Dell Disabilities and Orphanage Home • Ibadan, Nigeria

1. Commitment to Privacy

Bam Dell Disabilities and Orphanage Home ("Bam Dell Home", "we", "our") respects the privacy rights of our donors, volunteers, partners, and web visitors under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023).

2. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily submit to us through our contact forms, volunteer applications, newsletter signups, or donation verification forms (such as your name, email address, phone number, and location).

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To coordinate volunteer placements and background checks.
  • To issue acknowledgement receipts for financial and in-kind donations.
  • To respond promptly to inquiries regarding our children and home in Ibadan.
  • We never sell, rent, trade, or share your personal details with third-party marketers.
