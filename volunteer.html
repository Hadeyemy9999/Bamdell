Become a Hope Ability Volunteer

Use your time and talents to bring dignity, joy, and education to children with disabilities across Nigeria.

Open Volunteer Positions

We offer flexible full-time, part-time, and weekend volunteer opportunities in Lagos, Abuja, and remote settings:

🩺 Volunteer Physiotherapist / Occupational Therapist

Location: Lagos (Ikeja) or Abuja Center • 4–8 hours/week
Provide supervised physical rehabilitation and motor skills therapy for children with cerebral palsy and physical impairments.

📖 Braille & Sign Language Literacy Instructor

Location: Partner Schools (Lagos/Abuja) • Flexible
Assist visually impaired and deaf pupils with tactile reading, braille slate usage, and Nigerian Sign Language (NSL) practice.

📢 Community Outreach & Advocacy Volunteer

Location: Field Outreaches / Hybrid
Help organize community town halls, stigma awareness workshops, and parent support circle events.

🛡️

Child Safeguarding & Background Vetting

Because our volunteers work directly with vulnerable children, all applicants must undergo background vetting, reference checks, and complete our mandatory Child Safeguarding Code of Conduct training before field placement.
